Sharing the same sky
and weather with you

Integrates real open data from the Central Weather Administration (CWA). When it rains outside your window, your rice field gets rain too. Accompanied by day-to-night ambient white noise, it's your best companion for work and sleep.

Every grain of rice
has its own hometown

Based on your physical GPS location, the App will plant the most representative local Taiwanese rice variety for you. From Taikeng No. 9 in the North to Kaohsiung No. 139 (Ugly Beauty) in the East. Unlock exclusive local knowledge cards upon harvest.
